# Admission, scope, and coordinates

## What qualifies

**Current user policy (2026-10-17): popular multimedia franchises first, with explicitly labeled best guesses instead of skipping difficult lore.** The stricter evidence-gap dispositions from earlier batches are historical and are being reassessed. A plausible automation transition or technology-restraint comparison can be retained with `assessment_mode: "best_guess"`, `qualification_basis`, separate canonical facts and editorial assumptions. Missing household statutes or a canonical labor census are not reasons to omit a familiar case. This changes the admission certainty threshold, not the obligation to distinguish facts from inference.

A society needs an identifiable population and durable arrangements for production and access to necessities. Automation, autonomous machines, or a functionally equivalent technological production system must materially weaken the link between human labor and access to goods. Merely featuring an AI, an unemployed protagonist, rich individuals, or powerful weapons is insufficient.

The article also discusses societies that preserve employment despite automation. **The user explicitly confirmed on 2026-10-16 that these belong in the main database with a separate zero-variant label.** Once researched, record them with `record_type: "zero_variant"`, `regime_primary: 0`, and decision status `zero_variant`. They count toward the requested total of researched objects, while the progress file also reports the post-work subset separately. Dune, Nineteen Eighty-Four, and similar examples still need individual verification; appearing in the article is not itself proof. A conventional unequal society with no established automation connection remains a boundary comparison rather than automatically becoming a zero variant.

Potential candidates are deliberately broader than the final corpus. A title in the discovery queue asserts only a reason to investigate. `pending` means no lore verification has taken place. No candidate entry supplies an axis score.

## Units and duplicates

One record represents one society in a specified period, continuity, or realized story branch. Several books or adaptations depicting the same arrangements do not create several societies. Split only when control, guarantees, or labor arrangements materially differ, and document that difference. A game system's possible empire configuration can qualify as a modeled society only if explicitly labeled `player_configurable`; it is not an established canonical civilization. Hypothetical endings must be labeled as branches, not events that always happen.

A broad discovery candidate can yield more than one researched record when primary evidence establishes materially different periods or continuities. Its decision then supplies `society_ids` and a `split_rationale`; the legacy `society_id` is the first item. A mixed post-work/zero-variant decision has status `included`, while each record retains its own type and regime. An all-zero decision has status `zero_variant`. Processed candidates and retained societies therefore need not have equal counts. Splits do not change or reorder the frozen discovery queue.

After the original 784 candidates, continue in `data/candidates-expansion.json` with IDs c0785 onward. This is an append-only discovery queue, not a count of qualifying records. The loader verifies the original file’s frozen SHA-256 and contiguous IDs across both files. New metadata remains explicitly unverified until its decision; corrections to a lead’s identity or medium belong in its disposition/record rather than silently changing the frozen catalogue. Further works in a shared setting require a material institutional or continuity difference before another record is counted.

An elite enclave must disclose who is excluded. A machine society with no humans can illustrate autonomous control but cannot supply a human nonworking fraction: keep it as a comparison case until a distinct applicable denominator is justified. Extermination does not count as 100% human leisure. Alien and posthuman populations must be named explicitly; never silently count machine agents as humans.

For an active synthetic-person society with a meaningful continuing material order, an explicitly comparative record may use `scope.person_proxy_kind: "active_synthetic_persons"`. Its `axis_interpretation` must say that participation concerns synthetic persons, set `literal_human_nonworking` to `null`, and explain that negative control describes independence from human/organic owners rather than equal internal machine power. Energy, components, embodiment and maintenance are material needs; useful synthetic activity remains work. Do not multiply software threads, inactive copies or destroyed creators into persons. This makes autonomous-android and other synthetic societies reviewable without relabeling their inhabitants as humans.

A definite thematic non-fit can still receive a complete best-guess analysis. Save its `comparison_assessment` inside the decision, with scope, intervals, facts, assumptions and `counted_toward_goal: false`; the merger also exports [comparisons.json](../data/comparisons.json). Supply numeric best guesses for applicable axes. If a definite non-person ecology has no civilian person denominator, use a null participation value and interval with an explicit `not_applicable_axes` reason rather than inventing a human employment rate. This exception concerns completed outside-scope comparisons, not uncertain retained lore. Mark `current_policy_review: true` so it is not repeatedly treated as an unanalysed gap. Use this for an identified different mechanism, such as social-punishment voting, or a clearly uninhabited residual system. Do not use it merely because material lore is uncertain. Actual duplicate economic models remain linked rather than increasing the count.

A small contract-defined economic cohort can qualify when the text establishes an implemented, continuing automation-funded income arrangement, rather than merely a rich protagonist or a one-off windfall. Label that limited unit explicitly and do not extend its rights to surrounding workers or dependents. The Grammatizator's actual lifetime-contract authors are such a narrow case; automatic publishing by itself does not make the whole country post-work. An annuity is not proof of complete necessities coverage or of doing no other useful work.

## Axes

- `axis.guarantees`: -1 = reliable universal material provision; +1 = no reliable universal entitlement. Judge food, shelter, care, security of access, coverage, and exclusion. Comfort alone does not prove a guarantee. Political freedom is assessed separately from subsistence; coercion matters when provision is conditional or withdrawable.
- `axis.control`: +1 = broadly distributed human ownership and effective control; 0 = concentrated human elite control; -1 = decisive autonomous AI control. This is the article's ordered map, not a conventional political left/right axis. Government ownership alone does not establish +1. Autonomous AI can be plural and benevolent; neither makes it human control.
- `axis.nonworking`: 1 = no humans work; 0 = all humans work. This is an estimate of participation, not merely freedom from wage dependency. Assigned jobs and compulsory duties count even when inefficient, ceremonial or replaceable by machines; useful voluntary work and care for others also count. Private leisure and self-care are not automatically jobs. Use the scoped human population as the denominator and disclose population limitations. Under the current user instruction, provide a low-confidence best guess with a broad interval and explicit assumptions when no census exists. Never call an editorial percentage a canonical statistic. Do not convert an adult employment statistic directly into an all-age fraction.

Coordinates have plausible intervals and `high`, `medium`, or `low` confidence. Intervals are editorial uncertainty ranges, not statistical confidence intervals. Supply a central best guess with explicit assumptions when the lore leaves an axis uncertain; broad or competing institutional interpretations belong in the interval and alternatives. Historical nulls remain until individually reassessed. Numeric completeness means the record can be plotted, not that its estimates are canonical or independently verified.

A participation estimate based on an explicit service-age rule may use a disclosed illustrative demographic scenario. Store the scenario's noncanonical assumptions, calculation and allowance for useful activity outside formal service; do not label the result as a canonical census. A statement about people receiving income or the share needed in essential production is a separate statistic and does not automatically count all other people as nonworking.

Regime types follow the article: 1 concentrated/guarantees; 2 distributed/guarantees; 3 concentrated/no guarantees; 4 distributed/no guarantees; 5 autonomous AI/guarantees; 6 autonomous AI/no guarantees. Store an interpretive `regime_primary` and alternatives rather than deriving a spurious categorical truth from decimal thresholds.

A society can retain artificial jobs while already providing necessities independently of necessary wages. Record that overlap rather than creating two copies of the same economy. An optional `employment_preservation` annotation supplies `retained_artificial_jobs`, the interpretive `basis`, supporting `source_ids`, and `does_not_create_additional_society: true`. Scythe uses this annotation: its main type follows the realized AI-provision system, while its deliberately retained employment remains visible. This does not reclassify the existing separately labeled zero variants or imply that formal employment is a census of useful work.

Biological alien rulers over humans do not become AI merely because humans cannot overrule them. If the concentrated non-AI reference at 0 is used by analogy, disclose the ruling species, the analogy and its limitation explicitly, as with the Overlords in Childhood's End. A provisional regime label may remain while a control coordinate is unresolved; it must then be described as provisional, with the institutional gap visible.

## Evidence and publication

### Current research pace and priorities (2026-10-17)

Prioritize recognizable film, television, game, anime and comic franchises, reopening their earlier evidence-gap decisions before seeking more obscure novels. Use short references and existing gathered evidence. Missing details should produce an explicitly labeled best guess, not another `needs_evidence` disposition. In particular, regime 3/4/6 do not require a welfare floor: automation can coexist with insecure access. A zero variant may be an openly interpretive comparison of preserved labor and demonstrated technological capacity, without asserting a canonical job-protection motive.

Record `assessment_mode`, `qualification_basis`, `canon_facts`, `editorial_assumptions`, and a confidence label for each new best-guess assessment. Describe ending projections as projections, not enacted future legislation. Retain meaningful useful work in every denominator, and distinguish synthetic persons from non-person tools. Save explicit revisions under `research/revisions/`, including the complete prior decision or record. Reuse sources without changing their historical access dates; newly consulted sources get the actual current date.

### Historical research pace agreed on 2026-10-16 (skip rule superseded)

The user asked for analysis to run several times faster and explicitly allowed skipping obscure works that require reading the fiction to establish a fit. Use a reference-led pass: consult accessible lore summaries, reliable criticism, author/publisher descriptions and targeted official documentation. Secondary sources can establish a provisional record when they clearly describe the production and allocation arrangements; label them honestly and use broad estimates or nulls for unsupported axes. Do not begin a novel-length or elaborate format-decoding investigation merely to rescue an uncertain discovery lead.

The original fast pass recorded `needs_evidence` or `boundary_case` when a short reference did not establish the material economy. That omission rule is superseded by the 2026-10-17 instruction above. Historical dispositions remain auditable and can be revised. Deep primary-text reconstruction is still unnecessary.

Operational limit: start with one relevant short reference and use a second targeted check for a consequential contradiction. Failed title guesses and irrelevant search results are not lore evidence. If details remain uncertain, save a bounded best guess and continue. Do not turn a familiar lead into an open-ended reading assignment.

Use the work itself, author material, official episode guides, developer documentation, or publisher excerpts when readily accessible. Secondary reference works, criticism and fan documentation may establish sufficiently clear lore in the faster pass, but must be identified as secondary. Record which evidence supports which claim. A search result snippet is a discovery lead, not proof that the complete source was read. Preserve exact edition/episode/chapter scope when available.

Distinguish directly depicted facts, interpretation of those facts, and uncertainty. Descriptions should explain the axes in ordinary English, usually around 120–180 words across two short paragraphs. Do not invent labor statistics or read universal rights into a well-provisioned protagonist's home.

Statuses: `pending`, `researching`, `included`, `zero_variant`, `boundary_case`, `excluded`, `needs_evidence`, `duplicate`. A retained record can still have an unresolved axis. `reviewed` means a second pass against the gathered evidence, not independent peer review. A zero-variant label does not imply a literal 0% nonworking population: children and other nonworkers exist, and the same denominator rules apply.

Media are a nonempty array chosen from `book`, `short_story`, `movie`, `tv`, `anime`, `cartoon`, `manga`, `comic`, `game`, `tabletop`, `play`, `audio`, `web_fiction`, `webcomic`. Tag only media that actually depict this society; a franchise's unrelated adaptation is not enough.

## Completion review and counting scope

The completed reference-led stage is documented in `completion-review.md` and `completion-review.json`. Completion under the user’s best-guess instruction means every retained case has an actual bounded analysis, three usable editorial coordinates, source provenance, uncertainty and scope. It does not mean all inferences became canonical facts. The final review includes substantive controller-origin, work-denominator and medium corrections, rather than relying only on numeric completeness.

`scope-audit.json` provides a deliberately conservative resident-community subset for the 500-object minimum. A lexical selection excludes specialist, household and individual wording, even though many excluded records are valid communities or useful microcases. This is a counting check, not a lore classifier or a reason to discard uncertain cases. All retained records keep their actual population and limitations.

The completion marker is bound to SHA-256 hashes of the reviewed corpus and method inputs. Routine merge/validation cannot manufacture a new substantive sign-off after those inputs change. Do not rerun `write_completion_review.py` after a change without actually reviewing the new state.
