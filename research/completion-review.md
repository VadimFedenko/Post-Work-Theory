## Meaning of completion

Assistant case-by-case source-led passes plus targeted completion review; not independent peer review and not a guarantee that every fictional interpretation is uniquely correct.

- Coordinates and many institutional details are explicitly uncertain best guesses, as requested; they are not canonical statistics.
- Some records are projected or historical reconstructions, and some are narrow household/work/proxy comparisons. Their actual scope is not a universal claim about an entire franchise.
- Source references preserve what was read, including bounded indexes and criticism. This review does not claim all URLs are currently reachable or that the whole works were read.
- Conscious synthetic/collective person cases are comparative and keep their assumptions visible.
- Images remain deferred; no site or deployment is delivered.

Rebuild: `merge_batches.py`, `validate_data.py`, `update_checkpoint.py`, `audit_research_checkpoint.py`. The completion marker becomes stale if a reviewed input changes; a new substantive review is then required.
