# Article-aligned admission, scope and axes

Current policy: **article.txt**, taxonomy `article-2026-10-21`. The authoritative
definition is at lines 55–57: technological substitution makes labor cease to be
the main effective channel of distribution. The old methodology is preserved in
`article-alignment/baseline-methodology.md`; it is not the current classifier.

## Three different questions

1. How strongly does production still depend on work by actual persons?
2. Must the scoped population primarily earn material access through labor?
3. What share of that population actually undertakes useful work?

The second is the decisive post-wage criterion, with a meaningful automation or
equivalent technological connection. No threshold of nonworking determines it.
Qualitative production and wage-dependence judgments are stored separately in
`post_work_assessment`; they are not invented percentages of total output.

Commons, membership/status allocation, private productive assets, autonomous
allocation, patronage and techno-scavenging can all replace wages. A dystopian
post-wage system may destroy the earnings channel without a dependable substitute.
Ordinary unemployment in an otherwise wage-based society is insufficient. Market
trade or money can survive without wages being the primary distribution rule.

The zero variant is a retained comparison where the transition has not occurred,
technology is restrained or unavailable, essential person-labor remains central,
or an explicit uncertain best guess still favors earnings-based access. Traditional
communal sharing alone is insufficient without the technological connection.
Conscious robots, uploads and other active persons still perform real work.

## Current numbering

0 zero variant; **1 elite control with guarantees; 2 elite control without
guarantees; 3 distributed/shared control with guarantees; 4 distributed personal
productive assets without guarantees; 5 autonomous systems with guarantees;
6 autonomous systems without guarantees.** Historical types 2 and 3 were swapped.
Current fields, alternatives and analogies use the new numbers. Historical values
are explicitly versioned in `legacy_classification` and immutable batches.

Guarantees concern secure coverage and continuity of access, not merely a comfortable
snapshot. Private productive wealth is not automatically a universal nonalienable
share. Conditional support, caste exclusion, or threats to continued existence may
weaken a floor, but political unfreedom is not simply equated with no food.
System autonomy concerns effective control, not just the species or embodiment of
its components. Sibyl has an explicit functional type-5 reading and a human-brain
type-1 alternative; do not silently call it nonhuman software.

## Scope and coordinates

One record concerns one population, period, continuity and selected branch. Keep
actual endings, projections and configurable game systems explicit. Do not split
identical economic models just to increase count. `candidate_id` may link several
materially distinct records through `society_ids` with a rationale. Stable IDs do
not encode current type, even if they contain `zero`.

`axis.guarantees`: -1 reliable universal support, +1 absence of reliable universal
entitlement. `axis.control`: +1 distributed human control, 0 concentrated elite
control, -1 decisive autonomous system control. `axis.nonworking`: 0 everyone works,
1 nobody works. Participation counts useful voluntary creation, care, research and
compulsory assignments even without wages. It is a proportion of persons, not time,
productive output or formal wage employment. Dependents, inactive archives, dormant
copies, sleepers and dead populations must not be treated as idle workers.

Active alien, posthuman and synthetic societies require named person proxies and
explicit interpretation. Synthetic-only records preserve
`literal_human_nonworking: null`. The Institute now uses a mixed human/synth-person
denominator; it is not a census of born-human residents. The earlier scope and axes
remain available in revision 056. Narrow households, crews or institutions do not
establish a post-wage economy for their whole universe.

Every applicable coordinate has a central editorial estimate, interval, confidence,
basis and source links. Intervals are uncertainty scenarios, not statistical
confidence intervals. Regimes are not calculated from decimal cutoffs. Weak lore
receives an explicit best guess rather than an omission, following the user's
popular-multimedia-first policy. Do not read obscure works in depth merely to
resolve classification minutiae.

## Evidence, publication and persistence

Separate saved canonical facts, editorial assumptions and article interpretations.
Source records disclose access method, exact reading scope and limitations. No
claim of independent peer review or a full reread of every work is made.

The initial 890 main records have an explicit manual allocation choice in
`article-alignment/manual-classification.txt`, keyed by `article-alignment/review-index.json`. Their new judgments are
low-confidence. Revision 056 stores complete before/after records and decisions.
Main JSON exports are rebuilt from historical batches, ordered revisions, image
overlay and independently reviewed wellbeing overlay. The latter rejects stale
research until its assessment is reconsidered and logged.

Subsequent additions use the current taxonomy directly and carry a separate
hashed editorial review. The three Genshin Impact additions are documented in
`research/genshin-impact/`; they do not alter the historical 890-record migration.

The 29 supplementary scored records use another publication schema and have
`article_fit` judgments. The Futurological Congress book and Girls' Last Tour now
qualify substantively but remain supplementary, without fabricated finished-card
assets. Actual non-recipient mechanisms, failed plans or non-person ecologies can
still have scored analogies without an applicable post-wage classification.

The article is authoritative for the conceptual matrix, not evidence for every
plot assertion. Manna's contraceptive passage reports suspicion; Eleventh Voyage
reveals human operators; book/film BLAME! and Congress scopes stay separate. See
`data/article-examples.json` for exceptions and article-only examples.
